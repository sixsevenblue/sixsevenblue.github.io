Tools

Auto Rig

A Python system for building bipedal character rigs in Maya. Position the template geometry in an A- or T-pose and it builds the skeleton, sets driven keys, and connects the controls, leaving the rig open for further changes.

Copy Weights

My first Maya script, written after I moved vertices following skinning. It saves skin weights and re-applies them to a new mesh even when the vertex and face counts have changed.
